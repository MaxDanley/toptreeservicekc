About Stump Grinding & Removal in Lansing

A tree can be cut down, yet the stump can still cause trouble. With stump grinding, we remove it all. We grind about 4 to 6 inches under the soil so the area is smooth and ready for sod. You do not have roots sticking around.

Stumps do more than look bad. They can pull in termites and fungus. They also make mowing harder and can trip people with low spots. Plus, they waste room that could go to a patio, paths, or garden areas. Our crew brings the right tools for both small and large jobs, from one stump to many after a bigger tree was removed.

Each job includes grinding, taking away debris, and cleaning the site. When we finish, the yard is set for the next step. That might be sod, mulch, or a new bed for plants. We pay attention when we work close to fences and other plants, too. We do our best to keep your yard intact after we finish.

If there is a stump in your yard, it does not just stay put. It can cause more trouble over time. Grinding it down and taking out the roots helps a lot and usually gets the job done fast. After that, you get your space back.
