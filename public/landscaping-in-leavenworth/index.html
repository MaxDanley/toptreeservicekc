About Landscaping in Leavenworth

A good yard does not happen by accident. It comes from a plan. Our landscaping crew plans and builds new garden beds. We spread mulch, set in fresh plants, and refresh larger parts of your yard. In the end, we want your outdoor area to be simpler to enjoy and better to look at in every season.

We start by looking at the trees. That means we pay attention to root areas, soil health, and which plants will work together. This helps us avoid issues that can hurt older trees or lead to water problems later. Many lawn crews do not take this kind of care across the whole property.

We can handle small jobs too. Think new mulch, cleaned-up bed edges, and simple refresh work. We also do bigger makeovers when a full renovation is needed. Plant choices vary based on your soil type and sunlight. We provide proper guidance, solutions, and choices according to your landscape factors and budget.

Whether we are fixing one flower bed or redesigning a whole yard, our design experts visit your property to help you create a plan that matches your space and timeline. Planning is the foundation of any successful landscape project.
