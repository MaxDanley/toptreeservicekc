Our Four-Factor Evaluation Methodology

KC Tree Review applies the same evaluation framework to every Kansas City provider. This methodology focuses on factors homeowners can independently verify — not marketing claims or unsubstantiated ratings.

Public Documentation Review

We examine publicly available information including business registrations, insurance documentation, service descriptions, and operational history.

Scope Transparency Analysis

We analyze how clearly each provider communicates project scope, line-item pricing, cleanup standards, and timeline commitments.

Safety & Credential Verification

We verify whether providers publicly document crew training standards, insurance coverage, and safety protocols.

Response Quality Assessment

We evaluate communication speed, scheduling reliability, and customer service responsiveness.
