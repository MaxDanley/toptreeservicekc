About Stump Grinding & Removal in Leavenworth

A stump left behind is not just clutter. It can attract bugs, make mowing harder, and delay any fresh yard work. Our crew grinds stumps about 4 to 6 inches under the soil line. This clears the root area and leaves the ground level for sod.

Maybe you only have one stump in the backyard, or maybe you need help with many stumps after storm damage. Either way, we bring the right grinder. Smaller machines work well where space is tight. Heavier units handle bigger hardwood roots.

We take care of the grinding and also haul off the debris. After that, we finish with a cleanup at the site. That means you do not need a separate pickup or second visit.

Grinding also helps stop new growth. It limits rot issues and reduces the draw for termites that often come around old, untreated stumps. Many people ask for this step right after a tree job. It also makes the front of the home look better fast.

If you have an old stump, or more than one, in your Leavenworth yard, we can remove the stump material and clean up the roots. The area is then ready for what you plan next.
